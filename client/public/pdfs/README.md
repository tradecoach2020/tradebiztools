# PDF Upload Instructions

To upload your PDF files for the Free Trade Business Resources section:

1. Place your PDF files in this folder (`client/public/pdfs/`) with these exact names:
   - `whats-your-number.pdf` - for "What's Your Number"
   - `wealth-creation.pdf` - for "Wealth Creation" 
   - `property-for-dummies.pdf` - for "Property for Dummies"

2. The files will be automatically available for download when users submit the form.

1. Capture their contact information for your CRM
2. Automatically download the selected PDF file
3. Show a confirmation message
